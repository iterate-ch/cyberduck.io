You may have noticed that version 2.5.5 comes with a new crash reporter that will pop up a dialog asking to send me a report after relaunching the crashed application. It is much more lightweight than the previously used input manager from Unsanity (which you can deinstall from *~/Library/InputManagers/Smart Crash Reports* if you want) and has been developed by [Uli Kusterer](http://www.zathras.de/angelweb/blog-ukcrashreporter-oh-one.htm).
