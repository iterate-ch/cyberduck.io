Summary: Despite using the SpamFilter plugin, there were too many invalid tickets on the Cyberduck Trac in the last few weeks and your are now required to register before being able to open a new ticket or append comments.

Despite using the [SpamFilter](http://trac.edgewall.org/wiki/SpamFilter) plugin, there were too many invalid tickets on the [Cyberduck Trac](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/) in the last few weeks and your are now required to [register](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/register) before being able to open a [new ticket](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/newticket) or append comments.
