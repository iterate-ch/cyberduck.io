Supported by [Innovative Systems Institute](http://isi.ac.ge/), the Georgian (ქართული ენა) localization by Nelly Metreveli is the 31st translation of Cyberduck now available in the just released [3.4.2](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/milestone/3.4.2) update. Thanks to Aiet Kolkhi for coordinating the work.  
*Update: A known issues with missing menu shortcuts has been [reported](radar://7849807) to bugreport.apple.com*
