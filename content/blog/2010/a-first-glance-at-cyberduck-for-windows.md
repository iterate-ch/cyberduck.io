It is now a few days since the [announcement](http://sudo.ch/2010/08/25/cyberduck-for-windows-is-coming/) that Cyberduck is coming for Windows. We’ve got a bunch of positive reactions and many of you [signed up](mailto:feedback@cyberduck.ch?subject=Windows%20Private%20Beta) for the Private Beta Test. That’s very motivating and pushes us to put the finishing touches to the beta version. We’ll soon be ready and have also included the in-application software updater [wyUpdate](http://code.google.com/p/wyupdate/) that will allow us to run short development iterations during the beta phase with the same usability as in Cyberduck for Mac.

We are very excited to reveal some first screenshots!
