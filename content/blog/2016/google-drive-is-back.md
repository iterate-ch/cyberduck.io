Support for Google Drive is coming back in version 5 of Cyberduck. Access your files stored on Google Drive without synching to your desktop. You can give it a try in the latest [snapshot build](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/preferences#Update).

[![Google Drive](/2016/05/Google-Drive.png)](/2016/05/Google-Drive.png)

**Update: **Support for Google Drive is now also available in [Mountain Duck](https://mountainduck.io) with [version 1.4](https://mountainduck.io/changelog/).
