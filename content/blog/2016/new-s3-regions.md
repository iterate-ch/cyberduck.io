Summary: The latest update for Cyberduck adds support to create buckets in the newly opened AWS regions US East (Ohio), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), and Asia Pacific (Seoul) for Amazon S3. Update: Version 5.3 adds support for Montreal (ca-central-1) and London (eu-west-2).

The latest [update](https://cyberduck.io/changelog) for Cyberduck adds support to create buckets in the newly opened AWS regions *US East (Ohio)*, *Asia Pacific (Mumbai)*, and *Asia Pacific (Seoul)* for [Amazon S3](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/s3).[![aws-s3-regions](/2016/10/AWS-S3-Regions.png)](/2016/10/AWS-S3-Regions.png)

**Update**: Version [5.3](https://trac.cyberduck.io/milestone/5.3) adds support for Montreal (ca-central-1) and London (eu-west-2).
