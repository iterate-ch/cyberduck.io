Both [Cyberduck](https://cyberduck.io/) and [Mountain Duck](https://mountainduck.io/) have support for [hubiC](https://hubic.com/) (OVH), a low priced OpenStack Swift storage service. We have made available a [connection profile](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/hubic) that uses OAuth 2 for authentication to connect.[![hubic](/2016/11/hubiC.png)](/2016/11/hubiC.png)
