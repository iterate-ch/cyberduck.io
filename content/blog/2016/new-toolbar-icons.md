Summary: Version 5 of Cyberduck for Mac will feature new streamlined monochrome toolbar icons that match the appearance of OS X with small icons in toolbar buttons and segmented controls. You can give it a try in the latest snapshot build.

Version 5 of Cyberduck for Mac will feature new streamlined monochrome toolbar icons that match the appearance of OS X with small icons in toolbar buttons and segmented controls. You can give it a try in the latest [snapshot build](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/preferences#Update).
