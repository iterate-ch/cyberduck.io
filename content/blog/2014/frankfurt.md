Amazon Web Services has [announced](http://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-region-germany/) its second region in Europe opened in Frankfurt. Update to the latest [4.6](https://trac.cyberduck.io/milestone/4.6) [beta build](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/preferences#Update) or later to create and access [S3](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/s3) buckets in this new region. Support *eu-central-1* region required substantial work to implement the new AWS4-HMAC-SHA256 authentication version and SHA256 content checksum for uploads in the [Jets3t S3 framework](https://bitbucket.org/jmurty/jets3t/issue/183/support-for-aws-signature-version-4) used.
