There is another compelling reason to upgrade to the latest [3.2 release](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/milestone/3.2) of Cyberduck besides th[e usual enhancements and bug fixes](http://cyberduck.ch/changelog) the update provides. As a contributor to Cyberduck you receive a donation key that disables the donation prompt which is displayed periodically when quitting the application. Enter Cyberduck *Premium* Edition sort of.

When [contributing money](http://cyberduck.ch/donate) using Paypal, a key is automatically emailed to you. Contributors of [localizations](http://lists.cyberduck.ch/mailman/listinfo/cyberduck-localization), icon design or similar should request a donation key from me. Also if you sent me a DVD from Amazon please contact me as I do not receive your address from Amazon.

If you have donated previously to Cyberduck via Paypal and have not received a donation key, you can [recover](http://cyberduck.ch/donate/) your key.
