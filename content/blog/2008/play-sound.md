From [time to time](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/ticket/1963), someone opens a ticket in the Cyberduck [Bug Tracker](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/), requesting that a sound should be played once a download or upload is complete. I always close these tickets with a *wontfix/worksforme* resolution.

The workaround is to download [Growl](http://growl.info). After installation, restart Cyberduck and open the Growl Preference Pane in the System Preferences. Navigate to *Applications → Cyberduck → Configure… → Notifications → Select a notification (e.g. Download) → Choose Play Sound…*. Then pump up the volume of your speakers and transfer some files with Cyberduck.
