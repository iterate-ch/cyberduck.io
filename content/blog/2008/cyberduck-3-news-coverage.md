Summary: It’s great to see the widespread and positive news coverage about the release of Cyberduck 3 in macnn.com, macgeneration.com (Français), macplus.net , Macnews (Deutsch), tuaw.com and lifehacker.com and in many blog entries. MacTips.org even lists 9 Reasons to use Cyberduck. A longtime user of Cyberduck, schockwellenreiter.de (Deutsch), welcomes the addition of Amazon S3 protocol support.

It’s great to see the widespread and positive news [coverage](http://technorati.com/search/cyberduck) about the [release](http://lists.cyberduck.ch/pipermail/cyberduck-news/2008-May/000047.html) of Cyberduck 3 in [macnn.com](http://www.macnn.com/articles/08/05/14/cyberduck.3.adds.webdav/), [macgeneration.com](http://www.macgeneration.com/news/voir/130110/logiciels-en-bref-cyberduck-aliaskey-montage) (Français), [macplus.net](http://www.macplus.net/magplus/depeche-18192-cyberduck-passe-la-troisieme) , [Macnews](http://www.macnews.de/news/108263) (Deutsch), [tuaw.com](http://www.tuaw.com/2008/05/15/cyberduck-3-0/) and [lifehacker.com](http://lifehacker.com/382186/cyberduck-ftp-updates-with-quick-look-and-more-goodies) and in many blog entries. *MacTips.org* even lists [9 Reasons to use Cyberduck](http://www.mactips.org/archives/2008/05/16/9-reasons-to-use-cyberduck-for-ftp/). A longtime user of Cyberduck, *schockwellenreiter.de* (Deutsch), [welcomes](http://www.schockwellenreiter.de/2008/05/16.html#dieGummienteSprichtJetztMitS3) the addition of Amazon S3 protocol support. *MacUser* [emphasizes](http://www.pcpro.co.uk/macuser/news/197508/cyberduck-adds-idisk-and-s3-to-ftp-client.html) that Cyberduck can now mount .Mac iDisks through the addition of WebDAV support and *mackauf.de* (Deutsch) even concludes “[Ente Gut, alles gut](http://mackauf.de/nachrichten/3312/Cyberduck+3.0%3A+Ente+gut%2C+alles+gut/)“.

Both *[Infinite Loop](http://arstechnica.com/journals/apple.ars/2008/05/15/first-look-cyberduck-3-is-a-great-free-ftp-client-for-mac)* of ArsTechnica and [macapper.com](http://macapper.com/2008/04/21/cyberduck-3-flaps-wings-but-does-it-fly/) run a more extensive review of Cyberduck 3.

For Japanese users, both [E-WA](http://d.hatena.ne.jp/E-WA/20080515/1210779304#seemore) and [PISCES](http://pisces-319.seesaa.net/article/96753863.html) give an overview of the new features in 日本語 (Japanese).

Most [controversal](http://forums.cocoaforge.com/viewtopic.php?f=9&t=17078) is the [replacement of the bookmark drawer](http://sudo.ch/2008/03/19/new-bookmark-interface/). I am planning to imrove the [bookmark accessibility](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/ticket/1953) in the upcoming 3.1 release. Feedback and proposals are most welcomed.

The just released 3.0.1 bugfix release [addresses](http://trac.cyberduck.ch/query?status=closed&milestone=3.0.1) all important bugs that slipped into the initial 3.0 release.
