Cyberduck [6.3](https://cyberduck.io/changelog/) features support for [DRACOON](https://www.dracoon.com) – a enterprise cloud service made in Germany.

[![](/2017/11/dracoon.png)](/2017/11/dracoon.png)

> [DRACOON](https://www.dracoon.com) is a highly secure, platform-independent enterprise filesharing solution, which has repeatedly won awards as a market leader. It is certified according to the highest compliance guidelines. Unique to the solution is the own developed [TripleCrypt](https://support.dracoon.com/hc/de/articles/115001832709-Verschl%C3%BCsselung%20Verschl%C3%BCsselung) technology with an end-to-end encryption as well as the authorization system for high demands in the enterprise business environment.

The storage service is unique providing transparent, zero-knowledge client-side encryption for an enterprise file sharing environment.[![](/2017/11/DRACOON-Decryption-Password-Data-Room.png)](/2017/11/DRACOON-Decryption-Password-Data-Room.png)
