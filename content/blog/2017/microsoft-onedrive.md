The next major release of Cyberduck will support connections to [Microsoft OneDrive](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/onedrive). This includes support for both OneDrive Personal and OneDrive Business. You can give it a try in the current [snapshot builds](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/preferences#Update). Like our support for [Dropbox](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/dropbox), this allows to connect without synchronising files to your local computer.
