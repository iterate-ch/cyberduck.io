We have added support for [Google Team Drives](https://gsuite.google.com/learning-center/products/drive/get-started-team-drive/) in both [Cyberduck 6.3](https://cyberduck.io/changelog/) and and [Mountain Duck 2.2](https://mountainduck.io/changelog/).

> Google Team Drives are shared spaces where teams can easily store, search, and access their files. Unlike files in My Drive, files in Team Drive belong to the team instead of an individual. Even if members leave, the files stay exactly where they are so your team can continue to share information and get work done. Team Drives is available on [G Suite Enterprise, G Suite Business, or G Suite for Education editions](https://support.google.com/a/topic/4425947).

Use the regular Google Drive connection type to see the *Team Drives* besides *My Drive* and *Shared with me*.
