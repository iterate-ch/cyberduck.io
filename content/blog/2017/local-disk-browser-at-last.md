Update: We have updated our wiki

[Cyberduck 6](https://trac.cyberduck.io/milestone/6.0) will allow you to open a window to browse your local hard disk. Besides the obvious feature to drag files for download or upload to a remote server from within the application we have finally added a local browser to allow browsing [Cryptomator](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/cryptomator) vaults stored on your computer.

**Update: **We have updated our wiki with documentation to [access Cryptomator Vaults on your local disk](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/cryptomator#Accessvaultsonlocaldisk) with Mountain Duck or Cyberduck.
