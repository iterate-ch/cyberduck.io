Cyberduck [7.8](https://cyberduck.io/changelog/) and Mountain Duck [4.4](https://mountainduck.io/changelog/) improve support for Microsoft Sharepoint connections. The *Microsoft SharePoint Site* [connection profile](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/sharepoint#MicrosoftSharePointSiteconnectionprofile) now allows to connect to specific sites. The regular *Microsoft SharePoint* connection profile adds support to browse different libraries. Refer to our extended documentation for both [OneDrive](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/onedrive) and [SharePoint](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/sharepoint).
