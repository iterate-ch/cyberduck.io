The latest versions of [Cyberduck](https://cyberduck.io/) and [Mountain Duck](https://mountainduck.io/) now run natively on [Apple M1 hardware](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211814). Thus we are again shipping a universal binary on macOS since dropping support for PPC and Intel 32-bit (i386) in version 4.4 released in October 2013.

This was made possible by the work in [Rococoa](https://github.com/iterate-ch/rococoa), [JNA](https://github.com/java-native-access/jna) and [libffi](https://github.com/libffi/libffi).
