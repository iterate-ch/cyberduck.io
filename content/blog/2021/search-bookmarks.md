[Mountain Duck](https://mountainduck.io/) 4 introduced labels to assign to bookmarks to allow grouping in folders in the status bar and system tray menu. To further ease the lookup of bookmarks for power users with many connections we have now added a search field in the menu in version [4.4](https://mountainduck.io/changelog/) as known from the Cyberduck browser window.
