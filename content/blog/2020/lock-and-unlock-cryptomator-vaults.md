The latest version of Cyberuck and [Mountain Duck](https://mountainduck.io/) now allow to manually lock and unlock [Cryptomator](https://cyberduck.io/cryptomator/) vaults. This is useful as an alternative to the automated discovery of vaults with [configurable](https://trac.cyberduck.io/wiki/help/en/howto/cryptomator#Discovery) in *Preferences → Cryptomator → Auto detect and open vault in browser*.
