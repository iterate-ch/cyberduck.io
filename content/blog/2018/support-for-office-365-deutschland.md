Summary: You can now connect to OneDrive in Office 365 Germany in Cyberduck 6.3.0 and Mountain Duck 2.6.0 by installing the Microsoft Office 365 Deutschland OneDrive connection profile from our website.

You can now connect to OneDrive in *Office 365 Germany* in [Cyberduck 6.3.0](https://trac.cyberduck.io/milestone/6.3.0) and [Mountain Duck 2.6.0](https://mountainduck.io/changelog/) by installing the *Microsoft Office 365 Deutschland OneDrive* connection profile from our [website](https://cyberduck.io/onedrive/).
